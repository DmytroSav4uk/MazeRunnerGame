# Privacy Policy for MazeRunnerGame

**Last Updated:** February 21, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction
**MazeRunnerGame** ("the Application") is an educational project. We value your privacy and have designed this application to function without collecting, storing, or transmitting any of your personal data to external servers.

---

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect
* **No Personal Information:** We do not ask for your name, email address, or any contact details.
* **No Tracking/Cookies:** We do not use cookies or third-party tracking analytics (e.g., Google Analytics).
* **No Cloud Storage:** Your game progress is never uploaded to a database or cloud service.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage
While we do not collect your data, the Application stores information **locally on your device** to ensure a functional gaming experience:

* **Game Settings (Angular LocalStorage):** We use browser `localStorage` to save your preferences (e.g., volume levels, difficulty settings). This stays within your browser or desktop environment.
* **Save Files (Node.js/File System):** The desktop version of the application uses Node.js to create and read save files directly on your hard drive. These files contain only game-state information (e.g., current level, player coordinates).

---

## 4. GDPR Compliance (For EU Users)
Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), "processing" refers to any operation performed on personal data.

* **Data Controller:** Since all data stays on your device, you are effectively the sole controller of your data.
* **Legal Basis:** Any local processing is done based on **Contractual Necessity** (to provide the save-game functionality you expect) or **Legitimate Interest** (to remember your settings).
* **Your Rights:** Since we do not hold your data on our servers, you can exercise your *"Right to be Forgotten"* or *"Right to Access"* simply by deleting the save files on your computer or clearing your browser cache.

---

## 5. Data Security
Because your data never leaves your device, its security is dependent on the security of your own computer. We recommend keeping your operating system updated and your local files secure.

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy
As this is an educational project, this policy may be updated if new features are added. However, our "privacy-first" approach will remain the priority.
